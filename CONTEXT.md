# Tidder

A desktop Reddit client. This glossary pins down the words used for
Tidder's own concepts, and keeps them apart from Reddit's.

## Language

### Feedback

**Toast**:
A short, transient message Tidder shows in the corner of the window
to report the outcome of something the user did (success, warning or
error).
_Avoid_: Notification, snackbar, alert

**Notification**:
An item in the user's Reddit inbox (replies, mentions, messages),
as Reddit defines it.
_Avoid_: Toast, alert

### Posts

**Feed card**:
A post's entry in the feed: its title, score and thumbnail, never
its media.
_Avoid_: Post card, feed item

**Post viewer**:
The pane showing the one open post, with its media and comments.
_Avoid_: Post card, detail view

**Reddit video**:
A video Reddit hosts itself, whose sound is a separate track from its
picture.
_Avoid_: v.redd.it video, native video

**Video preview**:
A silent looping mp4 Reddit makes of a GIF or image post. It never
has sound.
_Avoid_: GIF, Reddit video
